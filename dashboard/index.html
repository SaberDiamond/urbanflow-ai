Who does what in Run 2
  • Nemotron 3 Nano (live, via Nebius Token Factory) recommends when ordinary signals switch. Feed: NEMOTRON.
  • Predictive Green Corridor is deterministic code: it times emergency priority from the ambulance's ETA and the queues. Feed: CORRIDOR.
  • Safety controller checks every model recommendation and adds yellow and all-red. Feed: SAFETY CHECK.
  • Local rules take over if the model fails or is slow. Feed: FALLBACK; the results say for how long.
  • The route is fixed for the comparison; the route planner is not used.
